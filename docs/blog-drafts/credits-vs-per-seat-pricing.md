# Credits vs per-seat pricing: how ATW bills

ATW bills a base hosting tier plus a shared Credits pool for usage, instead of making per-seat licenses the main meter for the core suite. Hosted mailboxes and AI work draw from that Credits pool. This post explains the model in plain language so buyers can compare it with classic per-user suites.

## The short version

- **Base hosting** covers the organization on ATW.
- **Credits** are the shared monthly usage pool for activity such as records, automation, hosted inboxes, storage footprint, messaging draw, and AI assistance.
- **Named users** on the core suite are not billed as the primary per-seat software fee in this model.
- **ATW Orbit** (Mail, Calendar, and Meetings today) ships with the ATW ERP plan. Messaging, Docs, Spreadsheets, and Presentations are Coming Soon.
- **Hub** is an Outlook-like client included with every plan.
- This article does not quote ATW dollar amounts or Credit rates. Use the live pricing page or a sales conversation for commercial numbers.

## What "per-seat" usually means

Many business suites charge mainly by active user licenses. Cost rises when you add people, even if usage stays light. That model is easy to understand and common for tools such as Zoho One and Google Workspace Business editions.

## What Credits are for

Credits collapse several usage categories into one customer-facing pool. Leadership can talk about capacity without reverse-engineering a long invoice of unrelated line items. When usage approaches the included pool, organizations can stop, opt into overage, or adjust hosting tier according to the product settings then in effect.

## Mail and AI under Credits

Mail is an ATW Orbit app. Creating and running hosted inboxes draws Credits. People who only need a named login without a hosted inbox are free named seats under the current marketing description. AI agents and related assistance also draw from the same organization pool rather than a separate seat type.

## When Credits help

Credits tend to fit teams that grow headcount faster than heavy platform usage, or that want ERP/CRM, mail, and AI on one organization bill. They also fit buyers who dislike paying a full seat for every light user.

## When per-seat may still fit you elsewhere

If your procurement standard is a fixed price per named user across a huge app catalog, or your team is standardized on a vendor that only sells that way, compare carefully. ATW publishes comparison pages for Zoho One, Odoo, Google Workspace email, HubSpot, Salesforce, and QuickBooks so you can see both approaches side by side.

## FAQ

### Are Credits the same as cloud credits from other vendors?

No. ATW Credits are ATW's usage unit for the organization pool described on the site. Do not assume another vendor's credit math applies.

### Do I pay per mailbox?

Mail is included; hosted inboxes use Credits. There is not a separate "mail seat" as the primary story in this model.

### Will you publish rates here?

No. Rates and dollar amounts change and belong on the live pricing experience or an order form, not in evergreen blog copy.
